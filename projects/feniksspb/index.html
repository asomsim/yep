Production

The production company FENIX LLC is a Russian manufacturer of cutting tools. The equipment fleet includes 5-axis CNC grinding and sharpening machines (Saacke, Walter) measuring machines (Zoller).

We manufacture both standard and special tools: End mills, Spherical cutters, Radius cutters, Reamers, Countersinks, T-shaped cutters, Drills, Profile cutters, Step drills, Chamfer cutters, Concave radius cutters, Engravers, Thread cutters and much more.
